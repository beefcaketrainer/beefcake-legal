BeefCake Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

In short: your workouts, measurements and partner details stay on your phone. BeefCake has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The only data that leaves your device is a random ID and error information sent to Expo, the service that delivers app updates.

1. Who we are

BeefCake is made by Remi Lord, an individual developer based in British Columbia, Canada. Contact: beefcaketrainer@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your device

This data is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. We never receive it.

DataWhy
Workouts, sets, reps, weights and personal recordsTo log your training and show your progress
Programs and exercises you addTo plan your training week
Body measurements (bodyweight and tape measurements)To track your physique over time
Accountability partner's name, phone number and message toneTo pre-fill a text to your partner
Settings (lbs or kg, awards already shown)To remember your preferences

3. Data that leaves your device

DataSent toWhy
A random app-install ID (not linked to you, your account or your device hardware)Expo (650 Industries, Inc.), our app update providerTo check for and deliver app updates, and to count update downloads
IDs of updates that failed to start, and the error text, only if an update failsExpoTo stop a broken update from loading again and to let us fix it

This happens over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Expo's own privacy policy is at expo.dev/privacy.

Things you choose to send. When you text your partner, share a record, or export a backup, BeefCake prepares the text or file and hands it to iOS. You decide where it goes: Messages, another app, or a folder you pick. BeefCake doesn't send it anywhere itself. "Watch a demo" opens a YouTube search for the exercise name in Safari, where YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

4. Permissions

BeefCake asks for no special permissions: no camera, photos, location, contacts, microphone, health data or notifications. Backups use the iOS Files picker, which only gives the app the file or folder you choose.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your phone stays until you delete it. To delete everything: Settings › Erase all data, or delete the app. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them until you delete them. Expo keeps update request records under its own retention policy; they contain no information that identifies you.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. The only third party involved is Expo, as a service provider for app updates.

7. Tracking and ads

BeefCake contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and does not combine your data with data from other companies.

8. Security

Your data is protected by your iPhone's own security, including its passcode and encryption. Update checks use HTTPS. Backup files are plain JSON, so keep them somewhere private.

9. Children

BeefCake is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Your rights

Because your personal data stays on your device, you can view, change, export or delete all of it in the app at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under Canada's PIPEDA, Quebec's Law 25, the EU and UK GDPR, or California's CCPA/CPRA: to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. To exercise any of them, email beefcaketrainer@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
